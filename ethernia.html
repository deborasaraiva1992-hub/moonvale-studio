✦   THE REALM OF LIGHT   ✦

ETHERNIA

Realm of Light, Hope and Forgotten Dreams

Where light remembers what the world has forgotten.

SCROLL TO DISCOVER
✦

On the other side of the Astrael Sea, where Fantasy's shadows give way to dawn, lies Ethernia — a realm bathed in eternal golden light. Here, the sun never truly sets, and every dawn carries the memory of the first light that ever touched the world.

Long before the chronicles began, Ethernia was a single radiant garden, born from the first ray of dawn. As ages passed, that light scattered into five sacred kingdoms, each carrying a fragment of the original brilliance, each guarding its own promise of hope.

Five realms rose from that light: Lumindor's celestial capital crowned in gold, Sylvarith's ancient worldtree whose roots remember every story ever told, Elarion Vale's eternal gardens where flowers bloom beyond the reach of time, Aurelith Veil's frozen peaks beneath dancing auroras, and Elynthara's golden shores where lighthouses guide ships home.

Each kingdom holds a piece of the same eternal truth — that light, once kindled, is never truly lost. To walk through Ethernia is to walk through hope itself, ancient and unwavering, waiting patiently for those who seek it.
